# 👥 Employee Attrition Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee Attrition Analysis is an HR data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel to explore employee turnover, workforce demographics, and factors that may influence employee retention.

The project analyzes employee information, including job satisfaction, salary, department, work experience, overtime, and attrition status to uncover insights that can help organizations understand workforce trends and improve employee retention.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information about:

- Employee demographics, including age and gender
- Departments and job roles
- Monthly income and hourly rates
- Job satisfaction and job involvement
- Years of experience and tenure at the company
- Overtime and business travel
- Education levels and marital status
- Work arrangements, including office-based work and working from home
- Employee attrition and employment status
- Training, promotions, and performance ratings

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

- Overall employee attrition rate
- Employee distribution by department
- Workforce distribution by gender
- Employee count by job role
- Attrition by department and job role
- Relationship between overtime and employee attrition
- Job satisfaction and employee retention
- Salary and employee experience analysis
- Employee tenure and years since last promotion
- Workforce demographics and employment trends

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand employee attrition patterns
- Identify departments and job roles with higher turnover
- Explore factors associated with employee attrition
- Analyze employee satisfaction and work conditions
- Evaluate workforce demographics and experience
- Provide data-driven insights to support HR decision-making
- Help organizations develop better employee retention strategies
